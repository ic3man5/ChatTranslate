It was generated by build_dict.py from the following sources:

- Spanish word frequency list from FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave, built from
  OpenSubtitles. CC BY-SA 4.0.
  https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords

- Spanish sentences from Tatoeba (https://tatoeba.org), used to find common
  phrases. CC BY 2.0 FR. https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/

- Translations produced with the OPUS-MT Spanish-English model by Jörg Tiedemann
  and Santhosh Thottingal (Helsinki-NLP), as packaged by Argos Translate.
  CC BY 4.0. https://github.com/Helsinki-NLP/Opus-MT
  J. Tiedemann and S. Thottingal, "OPUS-MT — Building open translation services
  for the World", EAMT 2020.

The words and phrases were selected, translated, filtered and supplemented with
hand-written entries; those changes are released under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license.
